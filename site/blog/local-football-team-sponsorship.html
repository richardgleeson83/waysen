Local Football Team Sponsorship

13 March 2024  ·  1 minute read

At Waysen and VanCatUK, we believe in giving back to the communities that support us. That's why we were thrilled to sponsor Whitfield Juniors — a local youth grassroots football team — to participate in a special tournament held at the iconic Crystal Palace stadium.

The event was organised by Precision Pass, who work to give young footballers extraordinary experiences. Thanks to VanCatUK's sponsorship, the children had the once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to experience the nerves, thrills and excitement of playing on a Premier League football pitch.

Walking out of that tunnel, hearing the crowd, feeling the pitch beneath their feet — these are memories that will stay with those young players for a lifetime. That's exactly the kind of impact we hope to make through initiatives like this.

"We were thrilled to be able to sponsor this event and look forward to many more opportunities like this in the future 🐈 ⚽️"

Community involvement is a core part of how we operate at Waysen. We are proud to support grassroots sport and the incredible young talent it nurtures across the UK.
